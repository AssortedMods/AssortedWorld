# Assorted Structures

Adds ruins, spires, pyramids, fountains, snowballs, water domes and sandstone pillars to find out in the world. Many of them hold one of sixteen runes, blocks that give you an effect when you stand on them or use them.

Also included in [Assorted World](../world). Worlds made with Assorted World 9.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
