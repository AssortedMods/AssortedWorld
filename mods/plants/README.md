# Assorted Plants

Adds gunpowder reed and glowstone seeds. Gunpowder reed grows wild by water like sugar cane and gives you a steady supply of gunpowder. Glowstone seeds hang from netherrack and ripen into glowstone.

Also included in [Assorted World](../world). Worlds made with Assorted World 9.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
