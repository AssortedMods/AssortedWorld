# Assorted Terrain

Adds randomite ore, which drops something different every time you mine it. Deserts get deep wells with a chest at the bottom of the shaft, fields of cactus and sand pits. The plains get wild crop fields and patches of melons, and the woods get stray saplings and old tree stumps.

Also included in [Assorted World](../world). Worlds made with Assorted World 9.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
