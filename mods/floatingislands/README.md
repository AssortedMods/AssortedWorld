# Assorted Floating Islands

Adds floating islands that hang high over the land. Each one takes after the biome below it, so you find snowy islands over the snowy plains, sandy ones over the desert and jungle islands over the jungle. Their stone has ore in it just like the ground does.

Also included in [Assorted World](../world). Worlds made with Assorted World 9.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
